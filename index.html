The product

Real ground, real ball flight

Most simulators ship a fixed list of courses. Greensmith builds new ones from real terrain, so players can play the course near home.

Courses from real terrain

Choose a location and Greensmith bakes the elevation, fairways, greens, bunkers and water into a playable course. Every green keeps its real slopes, so putts break the way they do outside.

Launch monitor ready

Hit real shots into a Uneekor or OpenFlight launch monitor. Support for more launch monitors is on the way.

Rounds for the group

Play full rounds with friends taking turns in the bay. The interface fits whatever screen the bay has, from a standard TV to an ultrawide projector.
